Not working? Step-by-step help

iPhone or iPad: tap Subscribe, then “Subscribe” in the Calendar window that opens.

Android or Google Calendar: tap “Copy link”. On a computer, open Google Calendar, click + next to “Other calendars”, choose “From URL”, paste the link and click “Add calendar”. It then appears on your phone.

Outlook: Add calendar, then Subscribe from web, and paste the link.

Children in two year groups? Subscribe once for each.

    Put this on your home screen

    Then it opens like an app, with its own icon. No app store needed.

    iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari (not inside WhatsApp, Facebook or an email).
    2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up. If you can’t see it, tap the … (three dots) in the bottom bar first, then Share.
    3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen (tap “View More” first if you can’t see it).
    4. Tap Add at the top right.

    Android phone

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) at the top right.
    3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
    4. Tap Install or Add.

    Ask for something to be added

    Spotted a missing letter, a wrong date, or something you can’t find? Tell us and it will be looked at when the app is next updated. Please don’t include children’s names or private details.
